# Phase 09 — Audio prototypes

**34/34 procedural prototypes · 52 audio files.** Synthesized from code with numpy and scipy and encoded as Ogg Vorbis (sound effects also as 16-bit 44.1 kHz WAV); no recorded material, sample library or music model was used. Levels, loudness, clipping, DC offset and loop seams were measured and each asset has a spectrogram, but nothing was auditioned by ear. Music is placeholder-grade. Loudness targets: ambience -28 LUFS, music -20 LUFS, sound effects peak -1.5 dBFS. Loops are seamless; enable loop on import. Rerun `tools/generate_phase09_audio.py` to regenerate.
